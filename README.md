# WILWATIKTA 3D — Cerita Jalanan 1998

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/apasi22/gta6-01-wilwatikta-3d-v20/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/apasi22/gta6-01-wilwatikta-3d-v20/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An original open-world crime game for the [gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01):
drive the red Garuda GT through the Javanese city of Wilwatikta at dusk, outrun the WPD,
fight rival gangs on foot, and climb from Fixer to legend across 12 story missions.

- **City**: 6 districts (Pelabuhan Segara, Kampung Batik, Lorong Emas, Pantai Kidul,
  Alun-Alun, Pasar Senja), window-grid towers, shop fronts, trees, lamps, zebra crossings.
- **People**: Sugeng Wibowo, Agus Prasetyo, Komisaris Wanto, Bambang the mechanic, Ratna,
  Slamet — with a full crew-hire row and dialogue.
- **Systems**: wanted stars with an IN SIGHT search zone, 5 weapon slots (Fist, Pistol,
  SMG Tawon, Shotgun, Rifle), car condition + bengkel upgrades, 3 radio stations
  (Gamelan FM 101.1, Wave 88.1, Celeste 98.1), a police heli with a searchlight,
  a transparent GPS map, and a 42% story ring.
- **Tech**: raw WebGL1, no libraries, zero network requests, runs in desktop Chrome.
  Serve locally with `node serve.ts` (or any static server) and open the game.

## Controls

WASD move/drive · mouse camera · E enter/exit car · click shoot · Q change weapon ·
0–4 weapon slots · M map · R radio · U bengkel · ESC pause.
